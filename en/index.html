How long does a divorce or khula case take?

Divorce duration varies: mutual consent divorce may take 1-3 months, fault-based divorce 6-18 months, khula 3-6 months. Duration depends on party cooperation, evidence documents, and court schedule. Al-Ghonemy lawyers expedite procedures by preparing strong documents from the start.

What are the land registry procedures for property in 6th of October?

Land registry in 6th of October: 1) Prepare official contract, 2) Pay fees and taxes, 3) Submit application to 6th of October or Sheikh Zayed Land Registry, 4) Document review, 5) Registration and receipt. Al-Ghonemy handles all procedures from A to Z.

How do I protect my inheritance rights if heirs refuse division?

If heirs refuse division: 1) File a forced partition lawsuit before Family Court, 2) Request court-appointed expert to evaluate estate, 3) Request property sale and proceeds division if physical division impossible. Al-Ghonemy specializes in inheritance & estates, achieving best results for heirs.

What are the steps to form a company in Egypt 2026?

Company formation in 2026: 1) Choose company type (Single Person, LLC, Joint-Stock), 2) Reserve trade name, 3) Prepare Memorandum & Articles of Association, 4) Deposit capital in bank, 5) Register in Commercial Registry, 6) Obtain Tax Card & Commercial Register, 7) Register in Social Insurance. Al-Ghonemy completes formation in 7-14 business days.
