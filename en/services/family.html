Quick answers to most frequent inquiries about family cases

Which court handles alimony cases?

The competent Family Court is the court of the wife's residence.

Can alimony amount be modified after judgment?

Yes, an alimony modification suit can be filed when circumstances change.

What is the difference between divorce and khula?

Divorce is initiated by the husband; khula is initiated by the wife with return of dowry.

Can custody be changed after judgment?

Yes, custody can be modified when the child's best interests require it.

What documents are needed for divorce?

Marriage contract, IDs, children's birth certificates, and proof of residence.

How long does a divorce case take?

Mutual consent divorce: 1-3 months; fault-based divorce: 6-18 months; khula: 3-6 months.

Can a wife get alimony after khula?

Khula typically involves waiving alimony, but child support remains mandatory.

Do you serve Sheikh Zayed Family Court?

Yes, we appear daily before Sheikh Zayed Family Court for all personal status cases.
