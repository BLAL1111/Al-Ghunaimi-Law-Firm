FAQ - Criminal Cases

أسئلة شائعة حول القضايا الجنائية والدفاع

Quick answers to most frequent inquiries about criminal cases

What is the legal deadline for cassation appeal?

Cassation appeal is filed within 60 days from the judgment date in criminal matters.

Can a lawyer be appointed after judgment?

Yes, a lawyer can be appointed to appeal the judgment within the legal deadline.

What is the difference between misdemeanor and felony?

Misdemeanor penalty is imprisonment up to 3 years; felony penalty is rigorous imprisonment, life imprisonment, or death.

Can an absentia judgment be appealed?

Yes, objection to absentia judgment can be filed within 10 days of notification.

What are prosecution investigation procedures?

Investigation includes hearing statements, confronting witnesses, and inspection. The accused has right to silence and right to counsel.

How is misdemeanor appeal filed?

Appeal is filed within 10 days of judgment, before Giza Court of Appeal.

What are accused rights in investigation?

Right to silence, right to counsel, right not to self-incriminate, right to know charges.

Do you serve 6th of October Public Prosecution?

Yes, we appear before 6th of October General and Partial Prosecution in all criminal case types.

What is criminal reconciliation?

Criminal reconciliation is a legal mechanism to resolve certain misdemeanors by agreement between parties with court approval.
